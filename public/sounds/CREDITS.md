## Replacing the placeholders

The plan (docs/06_tech_spec.md) says to use free, royalty-free library sounds. To swap them in:

1. Download a soft water ambience and a short plop from a free library, for example freesound.org. Choose sounds with the **CC0** licence, or with **CC BY** and credit the author.
2. Save them here as `ambient.mp3` and `plop.mp3`. The page uses `.mp3` first and only falls back to the `.wav` placeholders if there is no `.mp3`.
3. Replace the rows above with the real source link, author and licence of each sound.
