# Overview — Dreamy Fish Pond 1. What is it, in one sentence? A calm website where white fish swim in the middle of the sea, follow your mouse, and eat food that falls when you press a button. 

2. Who uses it, and what do they do? One person who wants to relax. They move the mouse around the sea and press the Freenove button to give the fish food. 

3. What does it notice: a press, a scan, a booking, a reading? 
Where the mouse is, and a press of the Freenove button. 

4. What does it do back, and where: on a screen, with a light, with a gate? On the screen. The fish slowly swim toward the mouse. When the button is pressed, food falls from where the mouse is, and the fish swim to it and eat it. 

5. What does the screen show before anyone does anything? A dreamy blue sea with soft light, and white fish swimming slowly around by themselves. A small line says "Move your mouse, press the button to feed." 

6. What are the numbers? How close, how long, how many. There are 5 fish. One press drops about 10 pieces of food. The food takes about 3 seconds to sink. If no fish eats a piece, it disappears after 5 seconds. There can be at most 50 pieces of food on the screen. 

7. What turns it on, and what turns it off again? The fish start following when the mouse moves over the sea. When the mouse leaves the page, they go back to swimming around by themselves. Food falls only when the button is pressed. 

8. What happens if a second person uses it while it is already running? 
There is only one mouse and one button, so they just take turns. If someone presses the button again, more food falls. 

9. What happens when something goes wrong, such as the connection dropping? If the button cable is unplugged, a small line says "Button not connected". The fish still follow the mouse. When the cable is plugged back in, the button works again. 

10. Does it keep anything after it is switched off? No. Every time the page opens, the sea starts fresh with 5 fish and no food.
